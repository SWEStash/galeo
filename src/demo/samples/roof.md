# Roof maths

The shed is $w = 2.4\,\text{m}$ wide with a pitch of $\theta = 25^\circ$. Each rafter runs from the
ridge to the eaves, plus an overhang $o$:

$$
r = \frac{w / 2}{\cos\theta} + o
$$

With $o = 0.15\,\text{m}$, $r \approx 1.47\,\text{m}$, so a $3\,\text{m}$ board gives two rafters.

The roof area, for the felt, over a length $l = 3.0\,\text{m}$:

$$
A = 2 \, r \, l \approx 8.8\,\text{m}^2
$$

and the number of shingle packs, each covering $3\,\text{m}^2$ with 10 % waste:

$$
n = \left\lceil \frac{1.1 \, A}{3} \right\rceil = 4
$$
